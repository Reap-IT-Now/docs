## Before you begin

Start with a small, agreed site or device scope. Confirm these items with the people responsible for REAP, network operations, and infrastructure in your organization.

| Area | Prepare |
| --- | --- |
| REAP access | Your organization's sign-in URL, an account or invitation, and permission to configure the relevant resources. |
| Deployment | A supported connector deployment environment and the installation instructions supplied for it. |
| Network scope | The first site, its management addresses or discovery ranges, and the intended connector placement. |
| Device access | Approved credentials and the permissions needed for the collection tasks you plan to enable. |
| Ownership | A person who can approve access, diagnose connectivity, and confirm discovered devices. |

## Network and firewall planning

Confirm the required destinations, ports, protocols, proxy behavior, and DNS requirements for your connector deployment before requesting firewall changes. The rules depend on the deployment and collection methods you use.

Ask your REAP representative for the supported sizing and network requirements for your environment. Do not substitute a generic firewall rule set for those deployment instructions.

## Choose your first success check

Pick one device or site you already understand. You will use it to confirm discovery and assess your first answer in [Chat](/capabilities/chat).
