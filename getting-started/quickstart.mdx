Connect one site, discover its devices, and ask your first question about the network. This path covers an **OnPrem** site using a connector hosted on **AWS EC2**.

## Before you begin

Complete the [requirements checklist](/getting-started/requirements). Have an approved initial discovery scope and a known device you can use to verify the results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access your organization">
    Sign in using your organization's REAP URL and confirm the active **Organization**. If you need an account or invitation, contact your REAP administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your site">
    In **Connectivity & Sites**, create the **OnPrem** site that will hold your network inventory.

    Follow [Create a site](/deployment/sites-and-discovery#create-a-site).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the site">
    Create a cluster, attach the site, and create a connector in that cluster. Download the connector's user data and deploy the REAP AMI in AWS EC2.

    Follow [Clusters and connectors](/deployment/clusters-and-connectors), including the registration and network readiness checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add and assign device credentials">
    Create the CLI (SSH) and SNMP v3 credentials your devices require. Create enabled assignments that match the intended site and device groups.

    Follow [Device credentials](/deployment/device-credentials).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a discovery job and start a run">
    Open the site's **Discover** tab. Save a job with your approved seeds or CIDR ranges and a **Manual** schedule, then start it from **Discovery Runs**.

    Follow [Create a discovery job](/deployment/sites-and-discovery#create-a-discovery-job). Saving a manual job does not start discovery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify a known device">
    Review the completed run, then open the site's **Inventory**. Check a known device's identity, management address, vendor, and model. Investigate missing devices and authentication or reachability errors before expanding the scope.

    Follow [Verify the results](/deployment/sites-and-discovery#verify-the-results).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first network question">
    Open **Chat**, select **Create thread**, and use **Send message** to ask a question. Replace the example site name with your own:

    > What devices have been discovered at the London site? Summarize the available device information and show the evidence behind your answer.

    Review the response and available evidence. Compare at least one result with the device you checked in Inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What success looks like

You can find an expected device, ask a question about the discovered network, and inspect the evidence supporting the answer. If information is missing, check the selected organization, discovery results, and available device data before expanding your deployment.
