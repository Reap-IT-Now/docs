A site organizes the network you want to discover. Discovery identifies devices and gathers the information you can inspect in Inventory and use in REAP workflows.

Create the site first. After you attach connectivity and assign credentials, return here to create a discovery job and start a run. This guide covers an **OnPrem** site.

## Create a site

1. Select the correct **Organization**.
2. Open **Connectivity & Sites**, select **Sites**, and select **\+** to open **Create Site**.
3. Enter a **Name** and set **Site type** to **OnPrem**.
4. Add the address, city, state, postal code, country, **Notes**, or **Labels** if useful for your team.
5. Select **Create Site** and confirm the site appears in the list.

## Prepare the site for discovery

Before you create a job:

- [Create a cluster, attach this site, and deploy a connector](/deployment/clusters-and-connectors).
- Confirm the connector can reach REAP and the intended management network, including the return path.
- [Create device credentials and enabled assignments](/deployment/device-credentials) for the target device groups.
- Agree on the initial seed management addresses or CIDR ranges, and identify known devices to check afterward.

## Create a discovery job

A **job** saves the discovery settings. A **run** executes those settings and records the result.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site's discovery workspace">
    In **Connectivity & Sites**, open your site and select **Discover**. In **Discovery Jobs**, select **\+**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the site and cluster">
    Verify **Site**, the inherited **Connector cluster**, and **Status**. Use **Active** for this job.

    The cluster comes from the site's attachment. If it is missing or incorrect, correct the association under the cluster's **Sites** tab before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter discovery targets">
    Supply approved addresses under **Seed devices or hosts**, subnet ranges under **CIDR ranges (optional)**, or both. Use one entry per line or comma-separated entries.

    At least one seed or CIDR is required for this OnPrem workflow. For subnet discovery, enter your approved subnet in the CIDR field; you can leave the seeds empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a manual job">
    Under **Schedule**, select **Manual** for your first run, then select the save icon in the top-right corner.

    Confirm the job appears in **Discovery Jobs**. The form also offers **Recurring**; first verify your discovery scope and results with a manual run.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start and monitor a run

1. Select the saved job in **Discovery Jobs**.
2. In **Discovery Runs**, select **\+** to open **Start discovery run**.
3. Review the selected job, **Site**, **Seeds**, **Targets**, and **Schedule**.
4. Select **Start Run**.
5. Open the run details and monitor its status, progress, and live events.

The run shows **Devices discovered**, **Devices updated**, **Unreachable**, and **Links discovered**. If you need to interrupt an active run, use **Stop run**, then review its state and available results before deciding whether to retry.

## Verify the results

A successful run reports **succeeded** and **100% complete**. Check the details as well as the final state:

- Review reported failures or unreachable devices.
- Open the site's **Inventory** and find devices you expected to discover.
- Confirm device identities, management addresses, roles, vendors, and models against your known environment.
- Compare the resulting inventory with the approved discovery scope.
- Open **Topology** to inspect available relationships. Its view can take additional time to populate after the discovery run completes.

Use the run details and Inventory as the completion checks. A summary device counter alone does not establish that all expected devices were discovered.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Job cannot be saved because a cluster is missing | Attach the site to the intended cluster, then reopen the job form. |
| A saved manual job has no results | Start a run from **Discovery Runs**; job creation alone does not execute it. |
| Devices are unreachable | Connector readiness, management addresses, routing in both directions, and protocol access rules. |
| Authentication errors appear | Credential values, device permissions, enabled assignments, scopes, and filters. |
| Expected devices are missing | Job targets, run events, credential coverage for each vendor, and connector reachability. |
| Inventory is populated but topology is incomplete | Allow processing time, refresh, and inspect the run's link results. If it remains incomplete, contact support. |

When [contacting support](/support/contact), include the site, job and run identifiers, time, expected device scope, and relevant errors. Exclude credentials and bootstrap material.
