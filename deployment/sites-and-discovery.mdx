Use **Connectivity & Sites** to organize your deployment by network location and run discovery within an agreed scope.

## Before you begin

Prepare a [cluster and connector](/deployment/clusters-and-connectors), [device credentials](/deployment/device-credentials), and a small initial set of expected devices.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the site">
    Open **Connectivity & Sites**, select **Sites**, and use **Create Site**. Enter the site's details and confirm the connectivity that will serve it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the discovery scope">
    Open the site and select **Discover**. Use the discovery configuration appropriate to your environment and keep the initial scope limited to approved targets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run and inspect discovery">
    Start the configured discovery job. Inspect the run details and any reported failures before repeating or expanding the job.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the inventory">
    Open the site's **Inventory** view or the organization-wide **Inventory** page. Compare the results with known device names and management addresses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to verify

- Expected devices are associated with the intended site.
- Device identity and management addresses match your known environment.
- Authentication and reachability failures are understood.
- Available device details provide enough context for your first question.

## If devices are missing

Check the discovery scope first, then connector reachability and credential assignments. Keep the run identifier and failure details when [contacting support](/support/contact).
