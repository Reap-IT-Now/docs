Connect a site in four stages: define its scope, prepare connectivity, assign credentials, and run discovery. Each guide below owns the detailed procedure, so you can return to the relevant step when expanding your deployment.

The current walkthrough covers an **OnPrem** site with a connector hosted on **AWS EC2**. Start with the [requirements checklist](/getting-started/requirements) and obtain the deployment details approved for your environment.

## Setup sequence

| Stage | Guide | Check before moving on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Create the site | [Create a site](/deployment/sites-and-discovery#create-a-site) | The OnPrem site exists in the correct organization, with its required name, postal code, and country. |
| 2. Prepare connectivity | [Clusters and connectors](/deployment/clusters-and-connectors) | The site is attached to the cluster, the connector reports **online** with recent **Last seen** information, and the required network paths work. |
| 3. Configure authentication | [Device credentials](/deployment/device-credentials) | Saved credentials have enabled assignments covering the intended devices. |
| 4. Discover and verify | [Create a discovery job](/deployment/sites-and-discovery#create-a-discovery-job) | A run has completed and its results match the expected devices in Inventory. |

## Continue from your results

Use [Chat](/capabilities/chat#how-to-use-chat) to ask your first network question, or explore [Inventory](/capabilities/inventory) and [Topology and Path Tracer](/capabilities/topology-and-path-tracer) to understand the discovered network.

When adding another site, review its connectivity and credential coverage before reusing existing resources.
