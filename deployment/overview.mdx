Work through these guides in order for your first site. When you expand to another location, reuse the same sequence and verify each layer before running discovery.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare connectivity">
    [Clusters and connectors](/deployment/clusters-and-connectors) covers the resources you manage in **Connectivity & Sites**, connector enrollment, and readiness checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure authentication">
    [Device credentials](/deployment/device-credentials) covers credential types and the information to confirm before assigning them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define and discover your site">
    [Sites and device discovery](/deployment/sites-and-discovery) covers site scope, discovery, and the checks to make in Inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you expand

Use [Requirements](/getting-started/requirements) to confirm deployment and network prerequisites. Keep your first discovery scope small enough to verify against known devices.

Once you can find the expected devices, return to the [quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) and ask your first network question.
