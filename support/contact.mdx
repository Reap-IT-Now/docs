---

Contact your REAP representative or email [support@reapitnow.ai](mailto:support@reapitnow.ai).

## Include in your request

- Your organization name and the relevant site or device.
- The page or workflow you were using.
- What you expected and what happened.
- The time of the issue, including the timezone.
- Any relevant run, thread, incident, or discovery identifier.
- The troubleshooting steps you have already tried.

Include useful error messages or screenshots. Remove passwords, API tokens, private keys, and other secrets before sharing them.

## Security questions

For security questionnaires, audit materials, or vulnerability reports, contact [security@reapitnow.ai](mailto:security@reapitnow.ai).
