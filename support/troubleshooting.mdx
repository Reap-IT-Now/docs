description: "Narrow common setup problems before contacting support."
---

Start with the layer closest to the symptom. Keep the affected organization, site or device, and time of the failure with your notes.

| Symptom | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| You cannot access the intended organization | Confirm the sign-in URL and ask your administrator to check your membership or invitation. |
| A connector is not ready | Review its state and the supported deployment and network requirements. |
| Expected devices are missing | Check discovery scope, connector reachability, credential assignments, and discovery run results. |
| A network answer lacks expected context | Confirm the organization, device presence, collection availability, and the scope of your question. |
| Slack actions do not work | Follow the [Slack troubleshooting guide](/integrations/slack) and check the prerequisite integrations. |
| A workflow fails repeatedly | Record the visible error and relevant run identifier, then [contact support](/support/contact). |

## Collect useful context

Include the page or workflow, what you expected, what happened, and when it happened. Add a run, thread, incident, or discovery identifier if one is available.

Share screenshots or error text with secrets and unrelated customer data removed.
