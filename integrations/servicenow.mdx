The [Slack incident workflow](/integrations/slack) depends on a configured ServiceNow integration. Complete the ticketing setup before testing incident notifications and ticket assignment in Slack.

## Before you begin

Work with your ServiceNow administrator to identify the instance, integration account, required permissions, and the scope of the initial test. Confirm which REAP organization will own the connection.

## Setup guide outline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare ServiceNow access">
    Confirm the supported authentication method and the minimum permissions for the specific incident workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the REAP connection">
    Open **Integrations** and configure the ServiceNow connection using the fields provided for your deployment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the connection">
    Use the available connection test and review any reported failures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate the incident workflow">
    Use a controlled test incident to confirm the required direction of data flow and any intended ticket updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the collaboration channel">
    Follow the [Slack guide](/integrations/slack) to configure the relevant targets and account links.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Completion criteria

A test incident reaches the intended REAP workflow, the expected ticket context is available, and any permitted action produces the intended result in ServiceNow.

## Required source material

The final procedure needs supported authentication details, exact instance fields, required ServiceNow roles or scopes, event or polling setup, writeback behavior, and a tested failure-recovery example. Do not publish these steps as a complete installation procedure until those details are verified.
