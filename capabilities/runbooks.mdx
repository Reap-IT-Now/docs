Use **Runbooks** to organize reusable operational procedures and review their execution.

## The workspace

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Library | Find reusable runbooks and inspect their versions and required inputs. |
| Schedules | Manage recurring executions, including their version, inputs, and timezone. |
| Executions | Review progress, reports, and evidence from manual or scheduled work. |

## Run a reviewed procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a runbook">
    Open **Runbooks** and find the procedure in the library. Review its purpose, selected version, required inputs, and intended targets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the execution inputs">
    Supply the values required for the task. Verify the target scope before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and launch">
    Use **Run once** for a single execution, or **On a schedule** when recurring execution is appropriate. Complete any review or approval requested by the workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the outcome">
    Open the execution and review its status, report, and available evidence. Resolve partial or failed steps before treating the task as complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to verify

The execution used the intended version, inputs, and scope. Review the outcome rather than relying only on the fact that a run was started.
