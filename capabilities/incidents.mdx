Open **Incidents** to find REAP incidents and incidents associated with external ticketing sources.

## Before you begin

Confirm the incident source and the organization you are working in. If the workflow involves an external ticket, ensure the corresponding integration has been configured.

## Investigation workflow

1. Open **Incidents** and locate the incident you want to investigate.
2. Review its summary, source, assignment, and any linked external ticket.
3. Inspect the available network context and investigation evidence.
4. Identify the affected scope and any missing information before choosing the next action.
5. Use the appropriate collaboration or ticketing workflow to coordinate follow-up.

## Working from Slack

The [Slack guide](/integrations/slack) covers incident notifications, questions, and supported actions in Slack. Its ServiceNow incident workflow requires a configured ServiceNow integration.

Keep external ticket state and the network evidence in view when interpreting the incident. Confirm where a state change is applied before taking an action.

## What to verify

You can identify the incident source, locate relevant evidence, and understand the current assignment and external ticket context.

## Review before publication

Validate list filters, incident detail tabs, state transitions, permissions, and the exact relationship between REAP and each supported ticketing source.
