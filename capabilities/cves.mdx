Open **CVEs** in the navigation to reach **Vulnerability Assessments**. Use the available device and CVE context to investigate an exposure.

## Before you begin

Confirm that the relevant devices and identifying information are available in [Inventory](/capabilities/inventory-and-topology).

## Review an assessment

1. Open **CVEs**.
2. Find the relevant CVE or device.
3. Open an assessment and review its device and site context.
4. Check the evidence and the assessment's time context.
5. Confirm applicability and decide the next operational step with the responsible team.

## Understand the result

An assessment is most useful when you can connect it to the affected device and the available evidence. Missing inventory or configuration information can limit what you can conclude.

The page provides current and historical time selections. Verify the selected time before comparing an assessment with a change or incident.

## Next steps

Use [Change Management](/capabilities/change-management) to plan validation for a remediation change, or inspect [Running config](/capabilities/configuration-history) for the collected device configuration.
