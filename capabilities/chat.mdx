Chat lets you ask questions about your network in natural language, as you would in a conversation with an AI assistant. Describe what you want to understand, name the site or devices involved, and ask follow-up questions as the investigation develops.

REAP connects the conversation to the network information and tools available in your organization. It can look up context, collect supported device evidence, and summarize findings. This gives your team a starting point for an investigation without first assembling the answer across multiple device screens.

## What you can do

- **Understand your network.** Ask about discovered devices, a site, or the relationships available in your topology.
- **Investigate a specific question.** Describe the observed behavior and relevant time period. REAP can ask for missing details and gather evidence for supported checks.
- **Build on an answer.** Continue in the same conversation to narrow the scope, examine a finding, or ask for the evidence behind a conclusion.
- **Inspect the outputs.** Review the response and any supporting tables, reports, topology views, or collected evidence produced by the request.

An answer depends on the available data, device access, and supported operations. A response that identifies missing evidence gives you a useful next step for the investigation.

## Example: start broad, then focus

Start with a question such as:

> Which devices have been discovered at the London site?

Then narrow the investigation:

> For edge-router-1, check interface status and show the evidence for any interfaces that are administratively enabled but operationally down.

These are example prompts; replace the site and device names with names from your organization. When timing matters, state the time window and ask when the supporting data was collected.

## How to use Chat

<Note>
  **Draft guide.** The functionality is grounded in the product source. Validate these steps against your deployed REAP version before publishing this guide to customers.
</Note>

Confirm that the relevant devices have been [discovered](/deployment/sites-and-discovery) and that you are in the correct organization.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a conversation">
    Open **Chat** and select the **\+** button labeled **Create thread**. Enter the question you want answered, then select **Send message**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clarify the scope">
    Include recognizable site or device names. Answer any follow-up question REAP asks to resolve the target or missing inputs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the answer and evidence">
    Inspect the response, progress, and any generated outputs. Check which devices and time period the evidence covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue the investigation">
    Ask a focused follow-up. You can request the basis for a conclusion, narrow the device scope, or ask what information is still missing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the result

A useful answer addresses the intended scope and lets you trace the conclusion to its evidence. Distinguish observed facts from explanations that still need verification.

If an expected device is missing, check Inventory and discovery results. If a request fails, retain the thread or run identifier and failure time for [support](/support/contact).
