Open **Chat** to start a network conversation. A useful question identifies the site or devices, the behavior you care about, and the time period when it matters.

## Before you begin

Confirm that the relevant devices have been [discovered](/deployment/sites-and-discovery) and that you are in the correct organization.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a conversation">
    Open **Chat** and create a thread. Enter a focused first question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the question a scope">
    Include recognizable site or device names. For example:

    > For the London site, summarize the discovered devices and show the available evidence for your answer.

    Replace the example site with one in your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the response">
    Review the answer, reported progress, and any generated artifacts or supporting evidence. Check the relevant scope and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a follow-up">
    Narrow an unclear answer with a specific device, relationship, or time window. Ask which evidence supports the conclusion and where information is missing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What success looks like

The response addresses the intended network scope, and you can identify the evidence behind it. An answer that reports missing data can also be useful: use it to identify the next collection or discovery check.

## Troubleshooting

If the expected device is missing, check Inventory and discovery results. If a run fails, retain the run or thread identifier and the time of the failure for [support](/support/contact).
