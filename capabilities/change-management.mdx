Use **Change Management** to organize pre-change checks, post-change checks, and the evidence used to assess the result.

## Before you begin

Define the intended change, affected devices, expected behavior, and conditions that must remain unchanged. Confirm the network data and access required for the checks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    Start a new change validation. State the intended change and its target scope clearly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the plan">
    Review the proposed validation plan. Resolve missing inputs and confirm that the checks match your success criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run pre-checks">
    Collect and review the pre-change evidence before proceeding with the operational change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implement the approved change">
    Follow your organization's approved change procedure for the actual implementation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run post-checks and review the verdict">
    Collect post-change evidence, inspect the pre/post comparison, and review the verdict reasons and recommended next actions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to verify

Check both the intended outcome and the conditions that must remain unchanged. Confirm that the before-and-after evidence is complete enough to compare, and investigate unexpected differences. Keep incomplete or inconclusive checks visible when assessing the result.

Preserve the original pre-change evidence. Replacing it with a collection taken after implementation removes the baseline needed to assess that change. Retain the validation record with the operational change record.

## Troubleshooting

If a validation needs input or attention, inspect its reported status and collection details. Avoid interpreting missing or failed checks as evidence that a change succeeded.

## Review before publication

Validate exact action labels, supported change sources and profiles, prerequisites for each stage, retry behavior, and a worked example.
