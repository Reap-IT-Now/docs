Change Management helps you establish whether a network change achieved its intended result. Describe what you plan to change, and REAP prepares a validation plan that defines what to check and what evidence to capture before and after implementation.

Your team reviews the plan, captures a baseline, performs the change, and then captures the post-change state. REAP compares the observations against the plan and retains the results in a validation report. This gives you a consistent record of what was expected, what was observed, and what still needs attention.

## What you can do

- **Plan the checks from your intent.** Supply the proposed change and target devices. REAP resolves the scope, requests missing information, and proposes checks supported by the selected change type.
- **Establish readiness before implementation.** Capture pre-change evidence and check it against the plan's required starting conditions.
- **Validate the outcome.** Compare post-change observations with the expected result and any conditions the plan requires to remain unchanged.
- **Keep the evidence together.** Review the verdict, reasons, collection details, and before-and-after observations in the validation record.

Change Management performs validation and evidence collection. Your team implements the network change through its approved operational process. The checks available depend on the change type and supported device platform.

## Example: enable an interface

An example request is:

> Validate interface Ethernet1 on edge-router-1 changing from administratively down to up.

REAP prepares the supported checks for that scope. You review the plan, capture the original state, enable the interface through your change process, and run the post-checks. The report shows whether the observed state meets the approved checks.

The report's conclusion is limited to those checks. An administrative-state check alone does not establish end-to-end application connectivity.

## How to use Change Management

<Note>
  **Draft guide.** The functionality is grounded in the product source. Validate these steps against your deployed REAP version before publishing this guide to customers.
</Note>

Before starting, identify the affected devices, expected outcome, and conditions that must remain unchanged. Confirm that the required devices are reachable through REAP.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the proposed change">
    Open **Change Management** and select **New change**. Enter the **Proposed change**, including recognizable device names and the intended outcome, then select **Start change validation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve the plan">
    Select **Review validation** when the plan is ready. If more information is required, use **Provide input in chat** and answer the clarification. Review the scope and success criteria, then select **Approve plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture the baseline">
    Select **Run pre-checks** before implementation. Review the evidence and **Pre-change readiness**. **Ready** means the plan's blocking baseline requirements are satisfied. **Attention** means advisory checks need review. For **Blocked** or **Inconclusive**, resolve the failed requirement or evidence gap and rerun the pre-checks before implementing the change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implement the approved change">
    Perform the change through your organization's approved process. Preserve the original pre-change evidence as the baseline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture and compare the result">
    After implementation, select **Run post-checks**. Review the validation report, verdict reasons, before-and-after evidence, and recommended next actions. Retain the validation with your change record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the result

Check that the intended outcome and required unchanged conditions were actually assessed. Missing, failed, or inconclusive checks do not establish that the change succeeded.

If the validation needs attention, inspect collection details and the selected plan before retrying. A collection taken after implementation cannot replace the original pre-change baseline.
