Use **Inventory** for device records and **Topology v2** for network views. Together, they help establish the scope of an investigation.

## Before you begin

Complete [site discovery](/deployment/sites-and-discovery) and verify at least one expected device.

## Find a device

1. Open **Inventory**.
2. Search by name, vendor, model, or IP address.
3. Open the device record and check its identity, site, and management address.
4. Review the available details for the question you are investigating.

The device view can provide links to collected configuration and other available evidence. The information present depends on what has been discovered and collected.

## Explore a network view

1. Open **Topology v2**.
2. Choose the appropriate system or saved view.
3. Set the scope required by that view.
4. Inspect the relevant nodes, relationships, and layers.
5. Check the view's time context before comparing it with an incident or change.

## What to verify

You can locate the expected device and distinguish the scope and time of the view you are using. Treat a missing relationship as a reason to inspect discovery and collection coverage before drawing a conclusion.
