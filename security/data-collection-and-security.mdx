## Data collection scope

REAP's published security statement describes telemetry, configuration, and operational data as part of the service's data context. The specific collection scope should be established for your deployment and the workflows you enable.

Before connecting an environment, confirm:

- The devices, sites, integrations, and collection methods in scope.
- The permissions granted to connectors and integration accounts.
- The data fields collected by each enabled workflow.
- The applicable retention, deletion, and data-location arrangements.

## Security review materials

For questionnaires, audit materials, or questions about your deployment's data handling, contact [security@reapitnow.ai](mailto:security@reapitnow.ai). Refer to the current [security statement](https://reapitnow.ai/security) when describing certification or attestation status.

## Report a vulnerability

Send a description of the issue and reproduction details to [security@reapitnow.ai](mailto:security@reapitnow.ai). Avoid including passwords, private keys, or access tokens.
