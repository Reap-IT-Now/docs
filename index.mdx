Use REAP to investigate network incidents and ask questions about your network. These guides walk you through connecting your tools and using REAP in your team's workflows.

## Integrations

<Card title="Slack integration" icon="slack" iconType="brands" href="/integrations/slack">
  Connect your workspace, receive incident notifications, ask network questions, assign ServiceNow tickets, and run runbooks from Slack.
</Card>

## Need help?

For help with access, initial setup, or an integration, contact your REAP representative or email [support@reapitnow.ai](mailto:support@reapitnow.ai). Include your REAP organization name and a description of what you need help with.
